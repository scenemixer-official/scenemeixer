# SceneMixer product facts

Read from scenemixer.com's public pages and public pricing endpoint on 2026-09-26. Figures marked *credits* are SceneMixer credits, bought in packs or included with membership; credit packs and current prices are on https://scenemixer.com/pricing/.

## What it does

Paste a script or a novel → the text is parsed into characters, locations, props and episodes → reference sheets are generated for each → every episode gets a storyboard (segments and shots) → every segment becomes a video clip → clips are assembled into the episode. Each step is editable before the next runs; projects save automatically.

## Languages

- Interface: 16 languages — Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), English, Portuguese, Korean, Spanish, Japanese, Indonesian, Russian, French, Italian, Dutch, Arabic, Turkish, German, Ukrainian.
- Spoken dialogue: 17 options — Chinese (Simplified), Chinese (Traditional), English, Portuguese, Korean, Spanish, Japanese, Indonesian, Russian, Cantonese, French, Italian, Dutch, Arabic, Turkish, German, Ukrainian.

## Picture

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 landscape by default; 9:16 vertical selectable per project.
- Reference sheets: characters at 9:16, locations and props at 16:9.

## Credits, per step

- Script parsing: 4 credits per 1,000 characters (alphabetic scripts count 30% of that per character).
- Reference image: 7 credits each.
- Video: per second, by tier (table above). Episode assembly: 1 credit per second.
- AI script writing: 4 credits per 1,000 characters.

## Free on every account

- 50 credits on sign-up.
- 15 free reference images per account.
- The first project's episode-1 storyboard is free.
- One free video preview of up to 5 seconds per account.

## Limits

- Single-pass script parsing up to 30,000 characters; long scripts up to 100,000 characters go through a two-stage parse.
- Up to 100 characters and 100 props per project.
- Remake from an existing video: source up to 300 seconds.
